# Product Styles

Use this visual vocabulary only for product- or service-focused presentations. The names in these guides are the canonical style labels used by the product taxonomy. They suggest visual characteristics; they do not prescribe palette, typography, story, or product behavior.

- [Digital and structured](digital-and-structured.md): Product-led minimal, Corporate / institutional, Technical / data-forward, Swiss / typographic, Precision / clinical, or Industrial / utilitarian.
- [Human and crafted](human-and-crafted.md): Editorial, Luxury, Human wellness, Sustainable / nature-led, or Heritage / artisanal.
- [Expressive and immersive](expressive-and-immersive.md): Playful / colorful, Immersive / cinematic, Tactile / crafted, Retro / experimental, or Youthful / culture-led.

## Apply a style direction

Start from the product's function, audience, setting, and price point. Choose one dominant style and, only when helpful, one restrained supporting influence. Translate the direction into slide rules for composition, image treatment, spacing, hierarchy, and detail. Select color palette and typography independently. Check that texture, motion, and decoration do not interfere with function or readability. Use real examples only as inspiration for observable qualities, not as templates to copy.
