# Human, Premium, and Crafted Product Styles

These directions can convey narrative, care, premium service, craft, or material awareness. Use them only when the product, audience, and evidence support those signals; avoid implying provenance, sustainability, efficacy, or care claims that have not been verified.

| Style | Visual characteristics | Presentation use |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial | Strong hierarchy, asymmetric grids, deliberate image crops, restrained captions | Research, strategy, culture, portfolios |
| Luxury | Quiet composition, refined details, tactile imagery, deliberate pacing | Luxury commerce, hospitality, premium services |
| Human wellness | Calm rhythm, natural imagery, gentle curves, reassuring language | Healthcare, mental health, wellness, lifestyle |
| Sustainable / nature-led | Repairable or natural material cues, clear evidence of sourcing | Reuse, climate, food, outdoor products; avoid unsupported green claims |
| Heritage / artisanal | Craft details, material texture, verifiable provenance cues | Local makers, food, heritage goods; keep claims about origin verifiable |
