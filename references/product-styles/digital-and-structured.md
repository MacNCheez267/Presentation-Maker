# Digital and Structured Product Styles

These directions favor clarity, organization, functional detail, or precise evidence. They can suit digital products, institutions, technical systems, medical devices, and work equipment when their real use context supports that choice.

| Style | Visual characteristics | Presentation use |
|---|---|---|
| Product-led minimal | Generous space, precise grid, short copy, clear interface crops | SaaS, developer tools, focused feature stories |
| Corporate / institutional | Stable grid, clear section labels, evidence-led charts, consistent alignment | B2B, finance, policy, healthcare operations |
| Technical / data-forward | Crisp diagrams, precise labels, measured technical detail | AI, cybersecurity, infrastructure, analytics |
| Swiss / typographic | Strong baseline grid, disciplined alignment, clear information hierarchy, simple geometry | Information-heavy decks, design systems, education |
| Industrial / utilitarian | Honest construction details, robust geometry, durable finishes, direct labeling | Tools, logistics, infrastructure, work equipment |
| Precision / clinical | Clear surfaces, measured spacing, explicit information hierarchy | Medical devices, diagnostics, laboratory or safety products |
