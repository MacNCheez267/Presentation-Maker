# Expressive and Immersive Product Styles

These directions use expressive composition, imagery, texture, or era-specific cues. Match intensity to the audience and task; keep controls, product details, and evidence legible, and avoid assuming a demographic has one shared taste.

| Style | Visual characteristics | Presentation use |
|---|---|---|
| Playful / colorful | Expressive shapes, illustrations, varied compositions, energetic but consistent layout | Consumer apps, food, youth culture, gaming |
| Immersive / cinematic | Full-bleed imagery, large scale shifts, sparse text | Travel, entertainment, launches, brand stories |
| Tactile / crafted | Paper, grain, soft shadows, hand-made visual cues used sparingly | Food, craft, community, independent brands |
| Retro / experimental | Era-specific graphic motifs, deliberate texture, unusual grids, controlled visual surprise | Music, gaming, creative concepts |
| Youthful / culture-led | Candid imagery, modular graphic elements, expressive compositions | Youth culture, creator products, events; avoid assuming all young audiences share one taste |
