# Sans-Serif and Technical Pairings

Sans-serif pairings work especially well in technical presentations, product and developer decks, data-heavy reports, dashboards, and other settings where clear, efficient reading matters. 
The pairings below range from neutral and functional to geometric, compact, or bold. 
Treat each as a starting point: its character can inform the tone, but it does not dictate the industry or visual style.

Check body-text readability at presentation size, especially when using a condensed or heavy display face.

| Heading face | Body face | Mood and suitable use |
|---|---|---|
| HK Grotesk (Bold) | Inter | Technical and contemporary; product or developer decks |
| Proxima Nova (Bold) | Inter | Geometric and forward-looking; emerging technology |
| Cake Sans | Source Sans Pro | Clean and open; business or informational decks |
| IBM Plex Sans | IBM Plex Sans | Precise and technical; data, engineering, security |
| Nunito Sans (Bold) | Nunito Sans | Friendly and rounded; consumer or family audiences |
| Archivo Black (Bold) | Inter | Bold and energetic; launches, music, culture |
| Barlow (Bold) | Barlow Condensed | Compact display hierarchy; sports, events, dashboards |
| Montserrat (Bold) | Open Sans | Confident and familiar; business, campaigns, consumer brands |
| Oswald | Lato | Compressed, energetic headlines; sports and events |
| League Spartan | DM Sans | Graphic, assertive headings; launches and culture |
| Rubik (Bold) | Lato | Friendly and functional; services and education |
| Raleway (Bold) | Open Sans | Light, contemporary headings with neutral body; portfolios and events |
