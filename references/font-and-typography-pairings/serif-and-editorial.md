# Serif and Editorial Pairings

Serif and expressive display pairings work well when a presentation needs an editorial, literary, refined, or warm tone. 
They suit research and institutional decks, culture and food features, hospitality, fashion, wellness, education, and other story-led topics. 
For example, a research presentation might use a restrained serif headline with a clean sans-serif body, while a food or culture deck could use a more expressive display face.

Suitability depends on the language, screen size, content density, and brand direction. 
Keep decorative faces to short headings, use readable supporting text, and verify that the fonts support the characters and scripts in your content.

| Heading face | Body face | Mood and suitable use |
|---|---|---|
| Source Serif Pro (Bold) | Inter | Editorial authority; research or institutional decks |
| Baskerville Display PT (Bold) | DM Sans | Refined and trustworthy; premium or formal topics |
| DM Serif Display (Bold) | Source Sans 3 | Expressive editorial; culture, hospitality, food |
| Lora (Bold) | Lato | Warm and readable; wellness, education, lifestyle |
| Playfair Display (Bold) | Open Sans | Polished but approachable; hospitality and lifestyle |
| Merriweather (Bold) | Lato | Editorial warmth; education, community, long-form topics |
| EB Garamond (Bold) | Montserrat | Fashion-led and expressive; beauty and editorial topics |
| Abril Fatface | Source Sans 3 | High-impact display with calm text; food and feature stories |
