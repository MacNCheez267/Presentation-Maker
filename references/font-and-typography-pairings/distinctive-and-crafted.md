# Distinctive and Crafted Pairings

Distinctive and crafted type pairings work best when a presentation benefits from a recognizable voice, such as a creative pitch, a culture or education deck, an independent brand story, or an innovation presentation.
For example, a local food brand might pair Fraunces with Work Sans for a crafted but readable feel

Protect body-text readability, and avoid pairing a highly expressive heading face with another typeface that competes for attention.

| Heading face | Body face | Mood and suitable use |
|---|---|---|
| Fraunces (Bold) | Work Sans | Crafted and distinctive; food, makers, independent brands |
| Bricolage Grotesque (Bold) | Source Sans 3 | Lively, contemporary display with a neutral reading face; culture, education, or creative topics |
| Playfair Display (Bold) | Inter | Expressive editorial headlines with a compact, highly readable body; features, hospitality, or public-facing reports |
| DM Serif Display (Bold) | Work Sans | Warm, high-character headings with an open sans-serif body; talks with a personal or narrative tone |

These are candidate pairings, not bundled visual styles. Confirm font availability, language coverage, and readability in the chosen platform; use the checks in [Type System and Pairing Selection](type-system-and-selection.md).
