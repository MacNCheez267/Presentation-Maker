# Chart Types

Choose a visualization from the audience question and data structure. Prefer a sentence, table, or labeled diagram when it communicates the point more clearly. These guides describe common chart families; platform support and native editability must be verified in the chosen tool.

- [Category comparisons](comparison.md): compare values or rank groups.
- [Trends and change over time](trends.md): show ordered change and timing.
- [Parts and hierarchy](parts-and-hierarchy.md): explain composition and nested groups.
- [Distributions and relationships](distribution-and-relationships.md): show spread, association, and outliers.
- [Process and contribution](process-and-contribution.md): show stages, components, or progress.
- [Geography](geography.md): show location-based patterns.
- [Chart quality and platform checks](quality-and-platform-checks.md): apply shared accuracy, readability, and tool-support checks.
