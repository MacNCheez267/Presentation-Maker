# Applying a Style Across Slides

Translate a style into repeatable rules for composition, spacing, image or diagram treatment, and information hierarchy. Keep the visual system recognizable while changing each slide's composition to suit its content. A chart, lesson diagram, quotation, and conclusion do not need identical layouts.

## Practical process

1. State the audience and purpose the direction serves.
2. Choose observable visual characteristics: grid, density, image approach, shape language, and level of ornament.
3. Choose the palette and typography separately; check that they support the tone and remain readable.
4. Apply the system to title, section, content, data, and closing slides without repeating one template mechanically.
5. Preview representative slides and correct inconsistencies or readability problems.

A visual trend or category convention is a starting point, not a reason to override the user's brief, mislead the audience, or reduce legibility. Do not report a color, font, image, template, or layout as applied unless the selected platform's result confirms it.
