# Research and Findings

## Purpose

Present a question, a credible method, what the evidence shows, and what follows from it.

## Flexible story pattern

Question → method and evidence → findings → limitations → implications

## How to adapt it

Keep methods proportional to the needs of the audience while retaining enough detail to assess credibility. Separate results, interpretation, limitations, and implications. Avoid turning correlation into causation or overstating generalizability.
