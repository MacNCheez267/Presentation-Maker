# Proposal or Pitch

## Purpose

Persuade a specific audience to consider a recommendation or make a decision.

## Flexible story pattern

Audience need → opportunity → recommendation or solution → evidence → trade-offs → decision or next step

## How to adapt it

Make the audience need and requested decision clear early. Explain the opportunity, recommendation, evidence, trade-offs, resource or implementation needs, and realistic next step. Tailor proof to the decision-maker. Do not hide risks or force urgency unsupported by evidence.
