# Strategy and Executive Updates

## Purpose

Explain current status, choices, risks, and decisions in a concise sequence.

## Flexible story pattern

**Strategy:** Current state → challenge or opportunity → options and trade-offs → recommendation → roadmap and decision

**Executive update:** Main takeaway → progress against goals → evidence → risks or decisions needed → next steps

## How to adapt it

Put the main takeaway near the beginning. Show progress against goals, meaningful evidence, risks, dependencies, options, and decisions needed. Keep background subordinate to the decision; make dates and status definitions explicit.
