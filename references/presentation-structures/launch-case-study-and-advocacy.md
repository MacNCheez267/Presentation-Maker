# Launch, Case Study, Portfolio, and Advocacy

## Purpose

Use a story structure when the presentation centers on a product experience, a documented body of work, or a cause.

## Flexible story pattern

**Launch:** User problem → value → workflow or demonstration → evidence and limitations → adoption or next step

**Case study or portfolio:** Brief and context → role or approach → selected work → outcome and evidence → lesson or next step

**Advocacy:** Human or community need → scope and evidence → causes or barriers → proposed change → practical action

## How to adapt it

For a launch, connect user problem to value, demonstration, evidence, constraints, and a useful next step. For a case study, distinguish context, role, method, outcomes, and evidence. For advocacy, present the need and scope fairly, show evidence and barriers, then propose a practical change or action. Avoid unsupported impact claims or calls to action without a meaningful audience role.
