# Brand and Visual Identity

## Purpose

Explain a brand or visual identity system and show how it should be used.

## Flexible story pattern

Audience and positioning → visual concept → palette and type → applications → rationale and next steps


## How to adapt it

Show audience and positioning, concept, system components, examples of application, and rationale. Explain the identity rationale and show a small number of representative applications. Palette and typography are design choices, not fixed by the narrative structure.
