# Product Design or Concept Review

## Purpose

Help stakeholders understand a user need, evaluate design choices, and decide what should happen next.

## Flexible story pattern

User need and context → design principles → concept options → prototype or journey → feedback and trade-offs → decision

## How to adapt it

Define the user and situation, then connect design principles to concept options, journey, or prototype. Show evidence from research or testing when supplied; distinguish observed feedback from assumptions. Make trade-offs, accessibility needs, technical constraints, and unresolved questions visible. End with a decision requested or next validation step, not an invented success claim.
