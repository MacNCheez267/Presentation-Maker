# Lesson and Explainer

## Purpose

Teach an idea or skill in an order that supports understanding and recall.

## Flexible story pattern

Learning goal → concepts in a useful order → worked example or demonstration → practice → recap or check for understanding

## How to adapt it

State a learning goal in plain language. Sequence concepts from prerequisite to application, explain unfamiliar terms, and use a worked example or demonstration before asking for practice. Close with a recap or a brief check for understanding. Do not assume a grade level, curriculum, or prior knowledge that the brief does not supply; use an introductory level only as a reversible default.
